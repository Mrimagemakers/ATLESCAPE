# Crowd credits

Partygoers: Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library (https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox), MIT License, Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation. Models and motion-capture clips (dance, cheer, talk, idle, sit, walk) from the Turn Up Music cast (Mrimagemakers/Turnupmusic, people/).

The five ATL Nightclub Mogul regulars (Imani, Keisha, Tasha, Brielle, Jada) are made at runtime from these avatars. nightlife-crowd.js reshapes the body and repaints the outfit.
