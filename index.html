HOW TO PLAY

Make it to the top of the A

  • Pick a gig in a district. Each one is a career move: artist, DJ, producer, promoter, hustle, label exec.
  • Run until the clock hits zero. Steer around obstacles and grab the cash on the road.
  • Stay ahead of the police, street thugs, drug dealers, crime boss crews and shady promoters behind you.
  • Caught means you lose what you picked up, and more, depending on who caught you.
  • Buy property: studios, stores, venues, buildings and night clubs. They pay out after every gig.
  • Heat rises with risky gigs. Above 60 the police join every chase.
  • The end: buy the Empire Tower on Peachtree, then win The Takeover.
KeyboardArrows or A / D steer. W / S speed up and ease off. V cycles chase, first-person and rear aim. Press M to lock mouse aim or drag to aim. Click, tap the aim area or press F to fire. Q shoves. R reloads. Esc pauses. Enter selects.
ControllerLeft stick or d-pad steers, up and down set pace. Start / Options pauses. A / Cross selects, B / Circle goes back. LB / RB switch tabs. In a chase, Y / Triangle changes view, right stick aims, RT / R2 fires, RB / R1 shoves, and X / Square reloads.
TouchDrag the stick at bottom left to steer. Push it up to sprint.
